Finance Pulse - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Finance Pulse ("the app") is a free news and market-price reader. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.

What the app does

The app fetches public headlines from third-party RSS feeds (CNBC, MarketWatch, Yahoo Finance, CoinDesk) and cryptocurrency prices from CoinGecko. Headlines link to the original publisher. Content belongs to its respective owners. The app is for information only and is not financial advice.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and we run no servers that store user data. News headlines are cached only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example the EEA/UK), the app shows a consent form and a "Privacy" option so you can change your choice. See Google's advertising policy and Google's privacy policy. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data security and retention

No personal data is sent to us. Data handled by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change.
